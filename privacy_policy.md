# Privacy Policy for Aronno Keyboard

**Last Updated: February 20, 2026**

## Introduction

Aronno Keyboard ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our keyboard application on Android devices.

## Data Collection

**We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any of your typing data.**

Aronno Keyboard is designed with privacy as the foremost priority:

- ✅ **Zero data collection**: Nothing you type is sent to our servers or any third party
- ✅ **Local processing**: All features work entirely on your device
- ✅ **No tracking**: We don't track your usage, behavior, or typing patterns
- ✅ **No analytics**: No analytics services are integrated into the keyboard

## How the Keyboard Works

### Word Suggestions
- Powered by **local dictionaries** stored on your device
- Bengali and English word databases are embedded in the app
- Learned words (optional) are stored locally in an encrypted format
- No internet connection required for suggestions to work

### Voice Typing
- Uses **Google Voice Recognition** service (when enabled by user)
- Audio is processed by Google's servers, not Aronno
- We do not store, access, or transmit your voice data
- Voice typing can be disabled in Settings if you prefer not to use this feature

### Themes & Stickers
- Downloaded from our content delivery server when you choose to install them
- Only theme files and sticker image data are transmitted
- No personal information or typing data is sent during downloads
- All downloads happen only when you explicitly request them

## Permissions Explained

### Internet Permission
**Why we need it:**
- To download optional themes from the in-app store
- To download optional sticker packs

**What we DON'T use it for:**
- Collecting your typing data
- Tracking your usage
- Sending analytics
- Advertising

### Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO)
**Why we need it:**
- To enable voice typing feature (optional)

**What we DON'T use it for:**
- Recording conversations
- Storing audio files
- Sending audio to our servers

**Note:** Voice typing uses Google's Voice Recognition service. When you use voice typing, audio is processed by Google according to their privacy policy. Aronno Keyboard does not access, store, or transmit this audio data.

### Vibrate Permission
**Why we need it:**
- To provide haptic feedback when you press keys (optional)

This permission does not access any personal data.

## Data Storage

### What is Stored Locally
The following data may be stored on your device only:

1. **User Preferences**: Your settings choices (theme, keyboard height, sounds, etc.)
2. **Downloaded Content**: Themes and sticker packs you choose to install
3. **Learned Words** (optional): Word frequency for personalized suggestions
4. **Pinned Phrases**: Custom phrases you choose to save for quick access

### What is NEVER Stored
- Passwords
- Credit card numbers
- OTP codes
- Private messages
- Personal conversations
- Any sensitive information you type

## Security

- All local data is stored in Android's protected app storage
- Dictionary data uses optimized formats that are not human-readable
- No network transmission of typing data
- No cloud backup of typing history

## Third-Party Services

### Google Play Services
- Used only for app distribution and updates
- Used for Google Sign-In (optional, for profile features)
- We do not share your data with Google beyond what's necessary for these services

### Google Voice Recognition
- Used only when you activate voice typing
- Governed by Google's Privacy Policy
- You can disable voice typing in keyboard settings

## Children's Privacy

Aronno Keyboard does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 years of age. Since we don't collect any personal data from anyone, the app is safe for all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Notifying users through the app (for significant changes)

## Android Security Warning

When you enable Aronno Keyboard, Android displays a security warning. This is a **standard Android message shown for ALL third-party keyboards**, regardless of their privacy practices. This warning is not specific to Aronno - it's Android's way of reminding users to trust keyboard apps carefully.

Rest assured: Despite this warning, Aronno Keyboard does NOT collect your typing data.
